US County Heatmap

Scaled by size

Each blue shape is its county shrunk around its center; its area is proportional to the value. Grey outlines show full size.

Scaled around 50

A value of 50 draws the county at normal size. Above 50 (blue) it grows, below 50 (orange) it shrinks; area is proportional to value ÷ 50. Grey lines show true borders.
